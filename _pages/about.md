I am a full-stack developer and an accountant by training.

I graduated with two First Class Honours degrees in Accountancy and Business Analytics from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, and an MBA from Baruch College, CUNY.

I previously worked in Fujitsu’s cloud computing department in Tokyo and the FinTech Division of the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS). At MAS, I worked under [Toh Wee Kee](https://daweek.org/speaker/wee-kee-toh/) on Project Ubin, exploring distributed ledgers and digital currency for interbank clearing and settlement. I also worked on IT security audits in KPMG Singapore’s Digital Trust department. I have served on the executive committee of a private shopping mall in Singapore valued at S$800 million, alongside [Lawrence Leow](https://www.sgengrs.com/our-team) of Crescendas Group.

I am advised by several teachers, including Edward Li, Lin Peng, Michael Grossman, Svenja Dube, Hagit Levy, Diana Brown, and Yen-Hee Tong. My research on prediction markets and ghost jobs has attracted academic and media interest. I have spoken with reporters from The New York Sun and The Atlantic. My prediction-market research has been presented at FMA 2026 and universities including Peking University, Fudan University, and Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My Ghost Jobs paper has been presented at Baruch College and the University of Colorado.

Before I worked in audit, I worked as a florist, a tuition teacher, and a hairdresser. I have taught Accounting Information Systems and Data Analytics at Baruch College for eight semesters as a full instructor. I feel blessed that my teaching has been well received, as reflected in student feedback on public sites such as Rate My Professors and Reddit. I keep pace with developments in AI, listen to my students, and revise my syllabus each semester. My education research has also been accepted for presentation at the Pre-ICIS SIG ASYS workshop (2026) and the American Accounting Association’s AIS/SET Midyear Meeting (2027).
